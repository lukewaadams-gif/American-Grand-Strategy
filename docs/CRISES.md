# Crises: the Taiwan Strait (Island Chain) and the Strait of Hormuz

Two in-depth crises, built on the presidency's Situation Room (milestone 8). Both are in the presidency, and both are standalone scenarios. Asked and answered on 13 November 2026.

**Status:**
- **The Strait of Hormuz is built:** its design and numbers are in `HORMUZ.md`; what is not yet built is in `DECISIONS_TO_REVIEW.md`.
- **Island Chain is built,** in the presidency and on its own (as America or as China): its design, numbers and wiring are in `ISLAND_CHAIN.md`; what is not yet built is in `DECISIONS_TO_REVIEW.md`.

## Island Chain in the game

Island Chain is the Taiwan Strait wargame in `/home/user/src/IslandChain` (one 12,450-line Java file). It is ported, not copied: this version is less detailed than Island Chain, but keeps what makes a crisis deep to manage.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|

| The side | **The United States in the presidency.** The standalone scenario can also be played as China. |

## The Strait of Hormuz

A new in-depth crisis.

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Escalation | **A ladder with several ways in:** tanker seizures, harassment, mining, missile attacks on shipping or Gulf oil sites, and a declared closure. It can be reached from strikes on Iran's nuclear programme, sanctions or an incident at sea. |
| The president's tools | <ul><li>**Escorts and mine clearing** with allies.</li><li>**Strikes on Iran's coast:** anti-ship missiles, fast boats, drones, mine layers.</li><li>**Diplomacy and back channels:** the Gulf states, China, India, Europe, Japan, and envoys to Tehran through Oman or Qatar.</li><li>**Oil:** the reserve, coordinated releases, war-risk insurance, and the pipelines that bypass the strait.</li><li>**A full ground invasion of Iran** (added by you).</li></ul> |
| The invasion | **A campaign in phases.** Months of build-up and basing talks, then the invasion at theatre level (axes of advance, the air campaign, casualties and munitions), then occupation and insurgency with years of costs at home. Limited options too: seizing the islands in the strait, or a coastal strip. |
| Scale | **Daily turns on a flat map** of the strait and the Gulf: shipping lanes, transits, mines, forces and Iran's coastal sites. |
| The other side | **Iran's factions and proxies.** Iran's government and the Revolutionary Guards' navy act partly apart, with the Houthis and the militias in Iraq and Syria. Israel and the Gulf states have their own aims. |
| The economy | **Through the whole model.** A fifth of the world's oil and much of its LNG pass the strait. Flows, prices, war-risk insurance and shipping costs feed the structural economy (gas, inflation, growth, the Fed), the allies' economies and approval. |

## Shared crisis management

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| The president's crisis tools | **All four:** <ul><li>intelligence and warning (ambiguous or deliberately deceptive indicators, with the community's confidence);</li><li>National Security Council meetings with options papers and the advisers' split;</li><li>back channels and hotlines;</li><li>public messaging and Congress (the War Powers clock, briefings, funding).</li></ul> |
| When a crisis starts | **From the world's tensions.** The Situation Room's tensions, deterrence and events make a crisis more or less likely each week. |
| Standalone starts | **Presets of assumptions:** start weeks before the crisis, with Island Chain-style presets (base case, optimistic, pessimistic, custom) and a chosen year. |
| The look | **Like the Situation Room:** the game's dark, flat style and type, the same panels, charts and meters as the rest of the presidency. |
